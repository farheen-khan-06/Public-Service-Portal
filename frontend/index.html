About

A citizen-friendly platform for accessing essential public services.

About This Portal

The Public Service Portal is an integrated platform designed to make important public services easier to find, understand and access.

The portal brings together employment assistance, government schemes, women's rights and protection resources, career guidance, skill development and legal document assistance in one citizen-friendly platform.

It is designed especially for citizens and skilled workers who may face difficulties finding reliable information or accessing different services through multiple websites.

User Roles
  • Citizen — find skilled workers, request services, save workers, browse government schemes, access women's rights resources and generate legal documents.
  • Skilled Worker — create and manage a worker profile, receive service requests, manage bookings, showcase work and access career and skill-development resources.
  • Admin — manage users, workers, schemes, document templates, platform content, notifications, feedback and reports.
Main Modules
  • Job Assistant — connects citizens with local skilled workers and provides career and skill-development resources for workers.
  • Women & Rights — provides legal rights information, government schemes, career guidance, skill resources and legal document assistance.
  • Government Scheme Finder — helps citizens discover government schemes and provides personalized eligibility guidance.
